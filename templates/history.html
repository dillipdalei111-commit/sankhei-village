Our Sacred Temples

Sankhei is a land of devotion, home to six prominent temples that form the spiritual core of our community.

  • ✦ Baba Kapileswar Shiv Temple - The central shrine dedicated to Lord Shiva, attracting devotees from all neighboring districts.
  • ✦ Shree Raghunath Mandir - A peaceful sanctuary dedicated to Lord Rama.
  • ✦ Maa Ramchandi Mandir - Honoring the powerful goddess Ramchandi.
  • ✦ Maa Bhairavi Mandir - A historic site of immense faith.
  • ✦ Maa Khilamunda Mandir - Enshrined amidst natural beauty.
  • ✦ Shani Dev Worship - An important aspect of our weekly rituals.

Festivals & Traditions

Our village is defined by its grand celebrations. The biggest of them all is the Danda Yatra, an ancient and physically demanding festival dedicated to Lord Shiva and Goddess Kali, celebrated in the month of Chaitra.

Alongside Danda Yatra, Sankhei vibrantly observes all major Odia festivals including Raja Parba (the festival of womanhood and earth), Dasahara, and Maha Shiva Ratri, where the entire village gathers at the Baba Kapileswar temple for night-long prayers.
